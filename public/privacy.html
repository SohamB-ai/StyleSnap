Privacy Policy — StyleSnap by Omenova Studio

Last updated: September 2026

StyleSnap is designed with a strict, privacy-first architecture. We believe that your developer workflow and web browsing activity belong solely to you.

1. 100% Client-Side Operation

StyleSnap operates entirely within your local browser. All style extraction, CSS token parsing, color clustering, asset scanning, and markdown generation run strictly in your local device environment.

2. No Remote Collection or Transmission

StyleSnap does not collect, transmit, upload, or store any personal data, URLs, browsing history, or extracted design systems on external servers.

3. Local Storage Only

All extracted data and user preferences are stored exclusively on your device using standard browser storage APIs:

  • IndexedDB: Stores full extraction snapshots and local cached asset records.
  • chrome.storage.local: Stores your extension settings (e.g., appearance theme, default export format) and a 10-item history index.

4. Zero Analytics, Telemetry, or Tracking

StyleSnap contains no third-party trackers, analytics libraries, session recorders, crash telemetry, or advertising beacons.

5. No External Server Access

The extension makes no outbound network connections to any backend server. All export files (JSON, Markdown, ZIP) are synthesized locally in browser memory.

6. Data Management & Deletion

You have full control over your stored data. You can delete individual past extractions from the History tab or completely purge all stored records at any time by clicking Clear all history in the extension Settings panel.
